Before you pay a token

Token money traps come up constantly in renter communities: pay any token only against a written receipt with the owner's name and the exact terms for getting it back, and only after you have seen the draft agreement. Two scam shapes renters describe again and again in Indian city rental groups: being pushed to pay before visiting, and an "owner" who will not video-call from inside the flat. Both end the same way - walk away.
